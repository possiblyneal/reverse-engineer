## Purpose

Peer-reviewed literature on the science inside the BRAINtellect/B-2 device —
HIRREM (High-resolution, Relational, Resonance-based, Electroencephalic
Mirroring; later Cereset Research) — plus independent work on EEG→audio feedback.
Background for understanding and improving the EEG feedback the clone
reproduces; not a substitute for the recovered design/montage data.

## Ownership

- `README.md` — the Brain State / Tegeler program corpus (full index with
  citations). The `.md` files are full article text; the one committed `.pdf`
  (the supplement tone table) is an open-access original;
  `supplement_2013_tone-example/` holds the dominant-frequency→tone example
  (CSV, note-frequency mapping, tone sequence, and that table PDF). The 1-min
  audio sample and the two larger PDFs exceed the 500 KB commit guard and are
  linked from `README.md`, not committed (see Local Contracts).
- `related-reading.md` — independent EEG→audio mapping, closed-loop auditory
  stimulation, autonomic mechanism, and neurofeedback trial-design references
  found by web search; leads to verify, not downloaded.

## Local Contracts

- External published materials only; keep open-access copies here, link
  (DOI/PMC) to anything paywalled rather than committing it.
- Anything over the repo's 500 KB commit guard is linked (DOI/PMC/registry),
  not committed, even when open-access; keep any local copy out of Git under
  `tmp/literature-oversized/`.
- Cite these from findings docs (e.g. `audio-feedback.md`); do not
  restate their content into the findings docs.
- Record provenance and collection date for anything added.
